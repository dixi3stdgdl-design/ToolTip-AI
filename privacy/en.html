Privacy Policy and Legal Notice

ToolTip AI · tooltip-ai.com/privacy · Version 3.0 · Effective 30 September 2026

This document governs use of the ToolTip AI desktop software (Assistant, Aura, Translate and Voice), the website tooltip-ai.com and related services (the “Product”). By installing, accessing or using the Product you accept these terms.

Functional warning (not fine print). ToolTip AI is a bring-your-own-key (BYOK) AI utility. When you start an analysis, screenshot crops and extracted text are sent to the AI provider you configure (e.g. Google Gemini, OpenAI, Azure OpenAI, OpenRouter or Anthropic Claude). We do not run our own capture servers; that does not mean your data stays on your PC. Do not analyse passwords, bank data, IDs, health data or third-party personal data if you do not want that content sent to the API.

1. Controller and notices

  • Controller / Product owner: Dixi3 Lqbs (commercially ToolTip AI / Dixi3 Labs).
  • Official contact: dixstdgdl3@gmail.com
  • For personal security and anti-impersonation reasons, no postal home address is published. Formal notices go to the email above and, if applicable, the Microsoft Store / PayPal publisher account.

2. Purpose

The Product is a Windows utility that accelerates work with real-time information: it interprets UI elements, screen crops and copied text to explain, translate or read them aloud when you need them. It is not cloud storage, a keylogger or permanent surveillance. Capture and AI sending happen only when you start them.

3. Data that leaves your device

  • Screen crops (Ctrl+Shift+A), full capture or clipboard analysis when you request it.
  • UI text and Windows UI Automation metadata.
  • HUD follow-up chat questions if you use them.
  • Text to translate (password fields are blocked).

This travels over HTTPS to the API you configured, with your key. Dixi3 Lqbs does not receive copies of those images on its own servers. The AI provider’s privacy policy and retention apply. You are responsible for what you choose to analyse.

4. Data that stays on your device

  • API key: Windows DPAPI (user scope) in %APPDATA%\ToolTipAI\config.json.
  • Preferences and local log %APPDATA%\ToolTipAI\run.log (you can delete it).
  • Translate cache in %APPDATA%\ToolTipAITranslate\ if installed.

Real shortcuts: Ctrl+Shift+A crop · Ctrl+Shift+D radar · Ctrl+Shift+C settings · Ctrl+Shift+T Translate.

  • Payments go through PayPal (or other official channels). We do not store card numbers.
